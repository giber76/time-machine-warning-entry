Entry for [Funniest Time Machine Warning Label](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-time-machine-warning-f8add151) on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes a single original warning label (99 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> WARNING: Do not argue with your past self. He has rehearsed this fight for years, and you have not.

The joke turns a real hazard of time travel — meeting your duplicate self — into the punchline: your past self has had years to rehearse an argument you have not even started yet. Deadpan safety-notice face, one idea, with the surprise landing in the last few words.

```sh
node main.ts
```
